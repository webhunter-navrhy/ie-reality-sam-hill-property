About the property

Description property

Sam Hill PSR 004775 is delighted to present to the market this well maintained 3 bed 3 bathroom semi detached.

No.148 is located in Mullingar in a much sought after estate and orientation is south front facing.

Accommodation:

Downstairs:

On entry to the right is a freshly carpeted large living room which has a solid fuel stove.

Leading through the French doors from the living room is an open plan dining room come kitchen. Patio doors lead out into a large back garden with garden shed and a horticultural unit. Included in the sale in the kitchen are oven fridge freezer and the cabinets are maintained to a high standard with a L shaped counter top.

There's a downstairs WC under the stairs. Heating is oil and burner is serviced regularly and a new heating programmer installed.

Upstairs:

Directly at the top of the stairs is a large bathroom with shower. At the rear is a large double bedroom with wardrobes.

To the front is a single bedroom which could also be used as an office. There is a Large Bright Master bedroom carpeted at the front with Ensuite.

This property comes highly recommended for first time buyers and families.

The property is in close proximity to Mullingar Hospital. and there is also a short cut walking route through the estate to the hospital primary and secondary schools, Tescos and Pennys are closeby and the estate is a short walk to Mullingar Town and close to the N4 (50 mins to M50) and surrounding Link roads.
